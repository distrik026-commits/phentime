# PHENTIME — Global Agent Instructions

These rules apply to all work in this repository involving product photography, image generation/editing, marketplace creative, product descriptions, and visual assets.

## Mandatory zero-third-party-brand rule
- Never add or intentionally display a third-party brand name, logo, monogram, emblem, watermark, branded packaging, or brand-identifying text in PHENTIME promotional artwork.
- Inspect the dial, bezel, crown, case, caseback, hands, markers, strap, bracelet, clasp, buckle, packaging, accessories, background, reflections, and every text overlay.
- Never invent text or logos. Do not imitate distinctive third-party brand identity.
- Use only the owner's supplied original PHENTIME logo asset, without redrawing it or changing its proportions.
- Do not replace a third-party mark on the physical product with the PHENTIME logo or otherwise misrepresent the product.
- Preserve the real product's shape, colors, materials, proportions, and verifiable features. Do not fabricate specifications or make unsupported authenticity/official/authorized claims.
- If a visible mark cannot be handled accurately without misleadingly changing the product, stop and request a new photo angle or manual review.

## Release gate
A human must inspect the final image before publication. If any prohibited brand identifier remains, or the product is misrepresented, mark the asset `REJECT` and do not publish it. Use `REVIEW` if uncertain; only use `APPROVED` after the checks pass.

## PHENTIME default visual direction
When appropriate, use realistic product photography, a clean dark-luxury/minimal style, subtle background texture, natural reflections, and square 1:1 composition at 1080 × 1080 px for marketplace assets. Product accuracy takes priority over styling.

## Source of truth
Follow `design/PHENTIME-DESIGN-SOP.md` for the complete SOP and `design/PHENTIME-IMAGE-PROMPT.md` for the reusable prompt. If instructions conflict, prioritize truthful product representation, applicable law, and marketplace policies.
